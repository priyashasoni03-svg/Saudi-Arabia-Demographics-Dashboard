
# Saudi Arabia Population Demographics Analysis (2010–2022)

## 1. Project Introduction

Understanding population demographics is essential for effective business planning, public policy, resource allocation, and market expansion. Population size, regional distribution, nationality, and gender composition can provide valuable insights into a country's demographic structure and changing population patterns over time.

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyse population estimates across Saudi Arabia from 2010 to 2022. The interactive dashboard presents demographic information through visualisations that allow users to explore population trends by year, region, nationality, and gender.

The objective is to transform population data into an accessible visual format that helps users understand demographic patterns and compare population groups across different geographical areas and time periods.

## 2. Business Problem

Organisations operating in Saudi Arabia need reliable demographic insights to support decisions related to market expansion, workforce planning, infrastructure development, public services, and regional resource allocation.

However, population data can be difficult to interpret when it is presented only in raw tables or spreadsheets. Without clear visualisation, identifying changes over time, differences between population groups, and regional concentration patterns can become challenging.

This project addresses the problem by developing an interactive Power BI dashboard that consolidates demographic indicators into a single view, enabling users to explore population estimates and identify patterns that may support informed decision-making.

## 3. Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Analyse Saudi Arabia's population estimates from 2010 to 2022
- Examine population trends across different years
- Compare Saudi and non-Saudi population estimates
- Understand population distribution by gender
- Compare demographic patterns across Saudi Arabian regions
- Develop an interactive dashboard with filters for year, region, nationality, and gender
- Present complex demographic information through clear and accessible visualisations
- Demonstrate how demographic data can support business and strategic planning

## 4. Dashboard Overview

The dashboard contains several visual elements designed to provide a comprehensive view of Saudi Arabia's population demographics.

### Total Population KPI
Displays an overall population figure based on the dashboard's current aggregation and filter context.

### Population by Year
A tabular view compares Saudi, non-Saudi, and total population estimates across years, allowing users to examine changes in population composition.

### Population Estimates by Region and Nationality
A horizontal stacked bar chart compares population estimates across regions, distinguishing between Saudi and non-Saudi populations.

### Saudi Population by Gender
A stacked column chart compares female and male population estimates across Saudi and non-Saudi population groups.

### Population Estimates by Year and Gender
A column chart compares female and male population estimates over time, helping users explore differences between the two groups and changes in their estimated population sizes.

### Interactive Filters
The dashboard includes slicers for:
- Region
- Year
- Nationality
- Gender

These filters allow users to customise the analysis and explore specific demographic segments.

## 5. Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Dashboard development and interactive reporting
- **Data Visualisation:** Stacked bar charts, column charts, KPI cards, and tabular reporting
- **Data Analysis:** Year-wise, regional, nationality-based, and gender-based comparisons
- **Data Modelling and Aggregation:** Organising population estimates for meaningful comparisons

## 6. Methodology

### Step 1: Data Preparation
Prepared the population dataset for analysis, ensuring that the relevant demographic fields were available for reporting.

### Step 2: Data Organisation
Organised the data around key dimensions, including year, region, nationality, and gender, to support comparisons across different demographic groups.

### Step 3: KPI Development
Created an overall population indicator and a year-wise population table to provide a summary view of the data.

### Step 4: Data Visualisation
Developed charts to compare regional population estimates, nationality distribution, gender composition, and population trends over time.

### Step 5: Interactive Dashboard Design
Added slicers for year, region, nationality, and gender so users can interact with the dashboard and investigate specific population segments.

### Step 6: Demographic Interpretation
Used the dashboard to explore changes in population estimates, compare demographic groups, and identify patterns that could inform further analysis.

## 7. Key Analytical Insights

The dashboard supports the following areas of analysis:

### Population Growth Over Time
The year-wise visualisation shows an increase in total population estimates from approximately 24 million in 2010 to 32 million in 2022.

### Saudi and Non-Saudi Population Composition
The dashboard separates Saudi and non-Saudi population estimates, allowing users to compare the composition of the population and explore how the two groups contribute to the overall estimates.

### Gender Distribution
The gender visualisations allow comparisons between male and female population estimates, both across years and between Saudi and non-Saudi population groups.

### Regional Population Distribution
The regional chart highlights differences in population estimates across regions, allowing users to investigate geographical concentration and compare demographic composition.

### Flexible Demographic Exploration
The interactive filters allow users to examine individual years, regions, nationalities, and gender groups rather than relying only on overall totals.

## 8. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to transform demographic data into an interactive analytical dashboard. By combining KPI reporting, time-based comparisons, regional visualisations, and demographic filters, the dashboard provides a structured way to explore Saudi Arabia's population estimates from 2010 to 2022.

The analysis highlights an increase in the displayed yearly population estimates over the period and provides tools for investigating differences by nationality, gender, and region.

From a business perspective, these insights can serve as a starting point for market research, workforce planning, regional demand assessment, and resource allocation. Further analysis combining population estimates with income, employment, consumption, and industry-level data could help organisations develop more specific business strategies.

## 9. Potential Business Applications

- **Market Research:** Identify regions that may warrant further investigation for market expansion
- **Workforce Planning:** Examine demographic composition as one input into labour-market analysis
- **Regional Planning:** Support preliminary assessments of population distribution and service requirements
- **Business Strategy:** Combine demographic trends with customer and industry data to evaluate potential opportunities
- **Resource Allocation:** Use regional comparisons to guide further investigation into infrastructure and public-service needs
